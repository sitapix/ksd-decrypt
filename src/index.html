    Help

    Decrypted copies save beside the originals. Use More → Output folder to save elsewhere.

    Click a filename to open it, or its folder button to show it in a folder.

    Compatibility

    Supports old Android KeepSafe vaults with the original built-in key. Newer vaults and other keys won’t decrypt.

    Photos and videos keep their original format. Playback depends on your system’s codecs.
